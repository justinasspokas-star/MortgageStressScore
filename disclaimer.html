1. Not professional advice

The score and explanations on this website are for general educational purposes only. They are not legal, financial, tax, mortgage, lending, credit, foreclosure-prevention, or housing-counseling advice.

2. No guarantees

The calculator does not guarantee that you qualify for refinancing, loan modification, forbearance, repayment plans, state assistance, or any other mortgage-related option.

3. Contact your servicer

If you are behind on mortgage payments or worried about missing a payment, consider contacting your mortgage servicer as soon as possible. Your servicer can explain options available for your specific loan.

4. HUD-approved counseling

If you are under serious mortgage stress, consider speaking with a HUD-approved housing counselor. Housing counselors may help you understand your options, often at little or no cost.

5. Avoid scams

Be careful with anyone who promises to stop foreclosure, guarantees loan modification, or asks for large upfront fees for mortgage relief. Always verify organizations before sharing personal or financial information.

6. Use your own judgment

You are responsible for decisions you make based on your own financial situation. Always verify information with your servicer, lender, attorney, financial professional, or HUD-approved counselor.

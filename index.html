    Is MortgageStressScore a credit score?

    No. It is an educational mortgage stress estimate based on your inputs. It is not a credit score, lending decision, legal advice, or financial advice.

    Read disclaimer →

    Will lenders call me after I use it?

    No. The calculator does not ask for your phone number and is not designed as a lender marketplace or lead form.

    Read privacy promise →

    Are my calculator inputs stored?

    The calculator is designed to run in your browser. We use Google Analytics to understand site usage, but we do not sell calculator inputs to lenders.

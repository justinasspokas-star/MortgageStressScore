HOPP: the free Homeowner Protection Program

The New York Attorney General describes HOPP as a statewide network connecting homeowners with qualified housing counselors and legal services. Counselors can help gather documents and communicate with a mortgage servicer; legal aid providers may help with foreclosure cases and court settlement conferences. HOPP is not itself a promise of a new cash grant.

Contact HOPP: 1-855-466-3456 or visit HomeownerHelpNY.org. See also the New York Attorney General's mortgage help page. Ask for a counselor or legal provider serving your county.

Is NYS-MAP still open in 2026?

No new applications are being accepted at the time of this review. NYS-MAP previously offered zero-interest assistance loans to eligible homeowners facing foreclosure, with historic descriptions mentioning loans of up to $80,000. Those descriptions are not a current funding offer. The program administrator, Sustainable Neighborhoods, explicitly says it is not accepting applications. Homeowners should contact HOPP for alternative help rather than plan on NYS-MAP funds being available.

New York Homeowner Assistance Fund (NYS HAF)

New York State Homes and Community Renewal states that NYS HAF has closed and is no longer serving people from its waitlist. If you already received an award and need to discuss it, use the contact instructions on the official NYS HAF page. HAF should not be advertised as accepting new applications.

Free help in NYC, Long Island and upstate New York

New York City (all five boroughs)

The Center for NYC Neighborhoods coordinates foreclosure-prevention resources. NYC residents can contact HOPP or call 311 for local guidance. The NY Attorney General also lists 646-786-0888 for the Center.

Long Island: Nassau and Suffolk

Ask HOPP to connect you with a legal-services or housing-counseling provider in your county. Bring any foreclosure papers, loan statements and payment records.

Buffalo and Rochester

Ask the statewide HOPP hotline for Erie, Niagara, Monroe or neighboring county referrals. Confirm current appointment times with the referred organization.

Albany and the Capital Region

Use the statewide HOPP network for area-specific counseling and legal aid. If a court case exists, give the counselor your next hearing date.

New York foreclosure and settlement conferences

New York generally uses judicial foreclosure. Certain qualifying residential mortgage cases have settlement-conference protections under CPLR 3408. Eligibility and timing depend on the loan, property and case; not every homeowner or proceeding qualifies automatically. Court papers can contain important response deadlines. Do not assume a fixed two-, three- or seven-year period or that requesting a modification automatically stops a foreclosure.

  1. When you receive a summons: read it immediately, note service and response deadlines, and contact a foreclosure attorney or HOPP legal aid provider.
  2. Before a court conference: assemble bank statements, proof of income, mortgage statements, hardship explanation and recent servicer correspondence.
  3. During a review: ask whether a trial payment, modification, forbearance or repayment option is being evaluated and request written confirmation of any decision and deadlines.
  4. If a sale date appears: obtain qualified legal help promptly. A counseling appointment is not a guarantee that a pending sale will stop.

Questions to ask your mortgage servicer

Suggested phone script: “I own a home in New York and am having difficulty with my mortgage payment. Please review me for all available loss-mitigation options for my loan type. What documents do you need, what is the deadline, and is any foreclosure or sale date scheduled? Please confirm the process in writing.”

Keep copies of uploaded documents and a dated record of each call. For an FHA-insured mortgage, see FHA loss-mitigation options and our FHA application checklist. For payment difficulty in general, read what to do if already behind.

Deed theft and mortgage rescue scams

The New York Attorney General warns that deed theft can involve forged deeds or deceptive promises to “save” a home by transferring ownership. Never sign a deed over as part of an unverified mortgage rescue, and be cautious with upfront-fee guarantees. See the Attorney General's deed theft guidance and contact HOPP if you need free help.

Common New York mortgage assistance questions

Can I apply for an $80,000 NYS-MAP loan?

Not at this time. NYS-MAP's administrator says it is not accepting applications. The historic maximum is not a current loan offer.

Where can I get mortgage assistance for free?

Contact the HOPP hotline at 1-855-466-3456 for statewide nonprofit housing counseling or legal referrals. Your servicer can review loan-specific assistance options.

Will a foreclosure settlement conference automatically save my home?

No. A qualifying conference may provide a structured opportunity to work toward a resolution, but it does not guarantee a modification or stop every foreclosure action. Seek case-specific legal advice.
